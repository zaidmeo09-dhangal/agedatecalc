# Contact

We read every message. Whether you found a bug, have a tool suggestion, or just want to say hello, reach out.

## Email

**hello@agedatecalc.com**

We usually reply within a few days.

## Before you write

- **Found a wrong result?** Tell us the exact inputs you used and what you expected. Screenshots help.
- **Want a new tool?** Tell us what it should calculate. If enough people ask for it, we build it.
- **Privacy question?** See our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) first. It covers what little data this site touches.
