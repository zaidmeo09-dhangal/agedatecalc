      <h2><span class="licon">🧰</span> What the tools do</h2>
      <p>Our tools cover the date and time questions people ask most often. The Age Calculator works out an exact age in years, months, and days. The Date Difference Calculator counts the days between any two dates. The Days From Today Calculator adds or subtracts days, weeks, months, or years from any date, and the Countdown Timer tracks an upcoming event live.</p>
      <p>For work and planning, the Business Days Calculator counts work days between dates, and the Week Number Calculator finds the ISO week number of any date. The Leap Year Calculator checks any year, the Birthday Countdown counts the days until your next birthday, and the Time Duration Calculator works out the hours between two times.</p>
      <p style="margin-bottom:0">The Stopwatch times anything with lap splits, and the Time Zone Converter converts times across the world. Each tool runs entirely in your browser, so the dates you enter stay on your device.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="legal-card legal-summary">
      <h2><span class="licon">📐</span> How dates are calculated</h2>
      <p>All calculations follow the Gregorian calendar, the calendar used in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Canada. A normal year has 365 days, and a leap year has 366 days, with the extra day added as February 29.</p>
      <p>A year is a leap year if it can be divided by 4, except for century years such as 1900, which must be divisible by 400 to count as leap years. That is why 2024 was a leap year, 1900 was not, and 2000 was. Our tools apply this rule every time, so ages and day counts stay correct across February 29 and across year boundaries.</p>
      <p style="margin-bottom:0">Time zone and daylight saving calculations use current standard time and daylight saving rules. Clocks move forward in spring and back in fall in the regions that observe daylight saving, and our converter applies the rule in force on the date you enter, not a fixed offset. That matters most around the changeover dates, when a fixed offset would give the wrong time.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="legal-card legal-summary">
      <h2><span class="licon">✅</span> Sources and method</h2>
      <p>Every result is checked with real dates before it goes live. We test each tool with known dates, including February 29 in leap years, year boundaries, and daylight saving changeovers, and we check the results against official sources before publishing a tool or an update.</p>
      <p style="margin-bottom:0">If a calculation ever looks wrong, tell us through the contact page with the exact dates you entered. The AgeDateCalc Team reads every message and checks every report against the same method before fixing it.</p>
