# About AgeDateCalc

AgeDateCalc is a small collection of free date and time tools. It started with a simple observation: questions like "how old am I exactly?" or "how many days until my exam?" come up all the time, and the answers should be one click away.

## What we offer

Five focused tools, each doing one job well:

- **Age Calculator** for exact age in years, months, and days
- **Date Difference Calculator** for counting days between dates
- **Countdown Timer** for tracking upcoming events
- **Stopwatch** with lap times for timing anything
- **Time Zone Converter** for converting times across the world

Every tool is free, needs no sign-up, and runs entirely in your browser, which means your inputs are never sent anywhere or stored.

## How we keep it free

AgeDateCalc is supported by advertising. In the future, you may see ads on the site served through Google AdSense. Ads never affect how the tools work, and we do not sell or share personal data.
