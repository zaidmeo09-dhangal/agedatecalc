# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Short version: our tools run in your browser. We do not collect, store, or sell the dates you enter.

## Data you enter into tools

Age, dates, and times you type into our calculators are processed locally in your web browser using JavaScript. This information is never transmitted to our servers, stored in a database, or shared with anyone.

## Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies:

- **Strictly necessary cookies** that remember basic preferences, such as display settings.
- **Advertising cookies** set by Google AdSense and its partners, used to show relevant ads and measure their performance. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Ads Settings page.

## Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. Learn more in Google's Privacy and Terms.

## Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which tools are popular, so we know what to improve. Analytics data is aggregated and never linked to individual users.

## Children's privacy

Our tools are general-purpose and do not target children specifically. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
