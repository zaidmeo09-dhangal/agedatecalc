**Short answer:** [a common year](https://www.timeanddate.com/date/common-year.html) has 365 days, and a leap year has 366.

## When each applies

Most years have 365 days. Leap years, which occur roughly every four years, add February 29 for a total of 366. The most recent leap years were 2020 and 2024. The next one is 2028.

The full rule: [a year is a leap year if divisible by 4](https://www.timeanddate.com/date/leapyear.html), except century years, which must also be divisible by 400. That is why 2000 was a leap year but 1900 was not. Check any year with the [Leap Year Calculator]({{ '/leap-year-calculator/' | relative_url }}).

## 366 days in a year

**Short answer:** a year has 366 days only when it is a leap year. A leap year adds February 29, so February has 29 days instead of 28 and the year total rises from 365 to 366. A common year has 365 days.

Years have 366 days because Earth takes about 365.2422 days to orbit the Sun. Adding one day roughly every four years keeps the calendar in step with the seasons. A year is a leap year if it divides evenly by 4, except century years, which must also divide by 400. Check any year with the [Leap Year Calculator]({{ '/leap-year-calculator/' | relative_url }}).

### 366 days contract

A "366 days contract" means a contract period of 366 days, which is one leap year in length. It does not mean one calendar year in every case, because a calendar year is 365 days unless it is a leap year.

How a 366-day span works depends on whether it covers a February 29. A 366-day span that starts on January 1, 2028 ends on January 1, 2029, the same calendar date one year later, because the span covers February 29, 2028. A 366-day span that starts after February 29, for example March 1, 2028, ends on March 2, 2029, one day later than the same calendar date, because that span covers no February 29.

Worked example: a contract starting February 29, 2028 runs 366 days to March 1, 2029, not February 28, 2029. February 28, 2029 is only 365 days after February 29, 2028, because 2029 is not a leap year and has no February 29. So when a 366-day contract starts on February 29 itself, the end date falls one day past the February 28 anniversary.

## How many days into the year are we?

This is the question people ask mid-year: how many days into the year are we, or in other words, how many days are we in the year. Count the days from January 1 to the date you want. For example, on November 22, 2026, the day number is 326, which means 325 days have already passed and 39 days are left in the year. 2026 has 365 days, so day 326 is about 89.3 percent of the way through.

A classic memory aid is the knuckle trick. Make a fist and count the months across your knuckles, starting with January on the first knuckle. Every month that lands on a knuckle has 31 days; every month in a valley has 30, except February. It works because the 31-day months genuinely alternate that way, with July and August as the one place two knuckles sit side by side.

## The century rule in one line

The "divisible by 400" exception keeps the average year at 365.2425 days: 2000 had a February 29, but 2100, 2200, and 2300 will not. If you are computing spans that cross 2100, those missing leap days change the total. The full rules, with the 1900 vs 2000 proof, are in [what is a leap year]({{ '/blog/what-is-a-leap-year/' | relative_url }}).

## Why the answer is not exactly 365.25

Note the two numbers: 365.25 days is the Julian average, one leap day every four years, not the measured orbit. The measured tropical year is 365.2422 days. The leap year system (adding a day every 4 years, skipping most century years) averages the calendar year to 365.2425 days, astonishingly close to reality. The leftover drift amounts to about one day every 3,000 years.

## When the difference matters

- **Age calculations:** total days lived changes depending on how many leap days fall in your lifetime.
- **Contracts and interest:** a 366-day year shifts daily-rate math slightly.
- **Anniversaries:** events anchored to February 29 need a rule for non-leap years.
- **Loan and savings interest:** banks often compute daily interest as the annual rate divided by 365, even in leap years (the "actual/365" convention), while others use 366. On a large balance, that one-day difference is real money.
- **Subscriptions and warranties:** a one-year subscription bought on March 1, 2023 ends March 1, 2024, which spans 366 days because it includes February 29, 2024. Bought March 1, 2024, the same "one year" is 365 days.
- **Payroll:** salaried staff paid monthly earn the same per month either way, but daily-rate contractors working through a leap year log one extra billable day if they work February 29.

## A year is not always 365 or 366 days

Astronomers use several different "years." The tropical year (365.2422 days) is what the calendar tracks. The sidereal year (365.2564 days) measures one full orbit relative to the stars. A lunar year, 12 lunar months, is about 354 days, which is why lunar calendars drift about 11 days a year against the Gregorian calendar. And calendar history has oddities: when Britain adopted the Gregorian calendar in 1752, the year had only 355 days because 11 days were skipped outright. For every modern calculation, though, the answer stays 365 or 366.

## FAQ

**How many days are in 2026?**

2026 has 365 days. It is a common year because 2026 does not divide evenly by 4, so February 2026 has 28 days.

**How many weeks are in a year?**

A common year has 52 weeks and 1 extra day, since 52 times 7 is 364. A leap year has 52 weeks and 2 extra days. Under the ISO week system, most years have 52 weeks, but a year has 53 ISO weeks when it starts on a Thursday, as 2026 does, or when a leap year starts on a Wednesday, as 2020 did.

**How many days are in August?**

August has 31 days, in every year. It is one of the seven 31-day months: January, March, May, July, August, October and December.
