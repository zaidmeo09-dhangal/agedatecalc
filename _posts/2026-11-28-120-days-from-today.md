**120 days from today is Sunday, March 28, 2027, which is Easter Sunday 2027.** That is 17 weeks and 1 day from Saturday, November 28, 2026, with 81 US business days in the span. The count excludes today, the same after-today method used across this site. ## How the 120 days split across five months

The 120 days run through the end of November, all of December and January, all of February, and most of March. February 2027 has 28 days, so it adds a full four weeks and no extra days.

| Month | Days counted | Running total |
|---|---|---|
| November 2026 | 2 | 2 |
| December 2026 | 31 | 33 |
| January 2027 | 31 | 64 |
| February 2027 | 28 | 92 |
| March 2027 | 28 | 120 |

The two November days are November 29 and 30. December and January do the heavy lifting at 31 days each, and the last 28 days fall in March, ending on Easter Sunday itself. If you are planning around the new year, the halfway point lands in late January: day 60 is January 27, 2027.

## Milestones on the way: 30, 60 and 90 days

Four months is a long stretch to plan in one block, so it helps to break it into monthly checkpoints. Each one lands on a weekday, which makes them useful for work deadlines and bookings.

| Milestone | Date | Day of the week |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days | December 28, 2026 | Monday |
| 60 days | January 27, 2027 | Wednesday |
| 90 days | February 26, 2027 | Friday |
| 120 days | March 28, 2027 | Sunday |

The 30 day mark, Monday, December 28, 2026, sits between Christmas and New Year, a quiet week for most offices. The 60 day mark, Wednesday, January 27, 2027, is a good point to check progress on anything started in the new year. The 90 day mark, Friday, February 26, 2027, closes the last full month before Easter, so anything that must be finished before Easter weekend should be done by then.

## Weeks, weekdays and business days

The span breaks down as 17 weeks and 1 day, 85 weekdays and 35 weekend days. For work planning, the number that matters is 81 US business days: 85 weekdays minus 4 US federal holidays that fall on weekdays inside the span.

| US federal holiday | Date | Day of the week |
|---|---|---|
| Christmas Day | December 25, 2026 | Friday |
| New Year's Day | January 1, 2027 | Friday |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day | January 18, 2027 | Monday |
| Presidents Day | February 15, 2027 | Monday |

Christmas Day and New Year's Day both fall on Fridays, so each one makes a three day weekend. Presidents Day on Monday, February 15, 2027 is the last holiday before the target date, leaving about six working weeks between it and Easter Sunday. ## Why the date is Easter Sunday 2027

Easter moves every year. It falls on the first Sunday after the first full moon on or after March 21, so the date can land anywhere from March 22 to April 25. In 2027 the rule lands it on March 28, the earliest Easter since 2016, and 120 days after November 28, 2026 happens to be that exact Sunday.

| Year | Easter Sunday |
|---|---|
| 2026 | April 5, 2026 |
| 2027 | March 28, 2027 |
| 2028 | April 16, 2028 |

Easter 2026 was April 5, more than a week later in the year than Easter 2027, and Easter 2028 moves later again to April 16. The same 120 day count from a different start date would land on an ordinary Sunday in most years, so the Easter landing is a feature of this particular start date, not of the number 120. In the UK and Canada, the Easter weekend runs the same way, with Good Friday on March 26, 2027 and Easter Monday on March 29, 2027.

## A four month plan: November 28 to March 28

Four months is enough time to plan a trip, a project, or a move, as long as each month has one clear job.

- December 2026: use the holiday weeks for booking, not for starting. Flights and hotels for Easter weekend are usually cheaper when booked early, and the week of December 28 is quiet, so it is a good time to lock in travel and accommodation for late March.
- January 2027: start the work itself. The first full working week after New Year's Day is the cleanest start, and the January 27 milestone is the point to check that the plan is on track.
- February 2027: finish anything that needs other people. February has only 28 days and Presidents Day removes one Monday, so treat February 26, the 90 day mark, as the real deadline for bookings, orders and arrangements.
- March 2027: wind down and prepare for Easter week. Daylight saving time starts on Sunday, March 14, 2027, two weeks before the target date, so clocks go forward before Easter weekend. Good Friday is March 26, Easter Sunday is March 28, and Easter Monday is March 29, so plan travel around a four day weekend where it applies.

The single biggest mistake with a four month plan is leaving the Easter booking until March. Easter 2027 is early, and the gap between Presidents Day and Easter Sunday is only about six working weeks, so the booking window is shorter than it looks.

## How to count 120 days yourself

Add 17 weeks first, then 1 day. November 28, 2026 plus 17 weeks is Saturday, March 27, 2027, and one more day is Sunday, March 28, 2027. Weeks are the safe unit here because they always land on the same weekday, so the only step that changes the weekday is the extra day at the end.

The same trick works for any count: divide the days by 7, add the full weeks, then add the remainder.
