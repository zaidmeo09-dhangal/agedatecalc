## Why February gets an extra day

Earth takes about 365.2422 days to travel once around the sun, not a neat 365. If the calendar ignored that extra quarter of a day, the seasons would drift: after a century, the calendar would be about 24 days ahead of the seasons, and July would slowly slide into spring weather. The leap day fixes the drift by adding one day, February 29. The century exceptions in the full rules keep the fix accurate over centuries, so the calendar stays lined up with the seasons for thousands of years. That is why 2028, like 2024 before it, gets a February 29.

## Leap years from 2024 to 2032

The three leap years in this stretch are easy to list, and each one adds a single extra day to February:

| Year | Leap year? | February 29 weekday |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | Yes | Thursday |
| 2025 | No | No February 29 |
| 2026 | No | No February 29 |
| 2027 | No | No February 29 |
| 2028 | Yes | Tuesday |
| 2029 | No | No February 29 |
| 2030 | No | No February 29 |
| 2031 | No | No February 29 |
| 2032 | Yes | Sunday |

The gap between leap days is four years here: February 29, 2024 to February 29, 2028 is 1,461 days, and February 29, 2028 to February 29, 2032 is also 1,461 days. Each four year stretch holds one leap day, which is why the count is 1,461 instead of 1,460.

## Why 2028 is a leap year

2028 is a leap year because 4 goes into it exactly: 2028 divided by 4 is 507, and it is not a century year, so the century exceptions do not apply. Here is the check, step by step:

1. Divide by 4: 2028 / 4 = 507 with no remainder, so 2028 passes the first test.
2. Divide by 100: 2028 / 100 = 20.28, so 2028 is not a century year and the century exception does not apply.

Result: 2028 is a leap year, with 366 days and a February 29. Century years face one more test: they must be divisible by 400, so 2000 was a leap year and 2100 will not be. ## February 29 birthdays in 2028

People born on February 29 are called leaplings, and 2028 gives them a real birthday for the first time since 2024. A person born on February 29, 2024 turns 4 on February 29, 2028, even though four calendar years have passed. Someone born on February 29, 2000 turns 28 that day.

## Why 2028 matters for planning

Leap years change more than calendars. February 2028 has 29 days, so monthly schedules, billing cycles, and deadlines that fall on February 29 only exist in that year. Contracts and subscriptions that run from March 1, 2027 to March 1, 2028 span 366 days because they include February 29, 2028, while the same span a year later is 365 days. ## FAQ

**How often do leap years happen?**

Usually every four years. The Gregorian calendar adds 97 leap days in every 400 years, because century years are leap years only when they are divisible by 400.
