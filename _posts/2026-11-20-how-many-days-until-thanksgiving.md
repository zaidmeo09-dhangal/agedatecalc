## How many days until Thanksgiving?

Thanksgiving 2026 is 4 days away from November 22. The count is simple: November 23, 24, 25 and 26 are four days, and today is not counted. ## How to work out the date yourself

Find the first Thursday in November, then add 21 days. That lands on the fourth Thursday every time, because the first, second, third and fourth Thursdays are exactly seven days apart.

For 2026: the Thursdays in November are November 5, 12, 19 and 26. The first Thursday is November 5, and November 5 plus 21 days is November 26. For 2027: the first Thursday is November 4, and November 4 plus 21 days is November 25. The range follows from the same rule: if November 1 is a Thursday, Thanksgiving is November 22; if November 1 is a Friday, the first Thursday is November 7 and Thanksgiving is November 28.

## Why it is never the last Thursday

Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday, not the last Thursday, and the difference shows in years with five Thursdays. November 2028 has five Thursdays: November 2, 9, 16, 23 and 30. Thanksgiving 2028 is November 23, the fourth, not November 30, the last. November 2029 is the same: five Thursdays, November 1, 8, 15, 22 and 29, and Thanksgiving is November 22, a full week before the last Thursday.

The fourth-Thursday rule is law, not custom. President Lincoln proclaimed a national Thanksgiving in 1863, and Congress fixed it as the fourth Thursday in November in 1941. The 1621 harvest feast in Plymouth is the origin story most people know, but the date itself comes from the 1941 law.

## Days, weeks and business days left

From Sunday, November 22, 2026, the countdown reads 4 days, 0 full weeks, and 3 [business days]({{ '/business-days-calculator/' | relative_url }}). The three business days are Monday, November 23, Tuesday, November 24, and Wednesday, November 25. Today, Sunday, November 22, is not counted, and Thanksgiving Day itself is a federal holiday, so it is not a business day either.

That is the number that matters for errands and deliveries. Four calendar days sounds comfortable, but only three of them are working days, so anything that needs a weekday, a delivery slot, or an open office has to happen by Wednesday, November 25.

## Thanksgiving planning timeline: 4 days out

Four days is enough time to shop, thaw and prep without rushing, as long as the turkey moves first.

- Sunday, November 22: move a frozen turkey from the freezer to the fridge. A 12 to 16 pound turkey needs 3 to 4 days in the fridge, so one moved today is ready by November 25 to 26. Confirm the guest count and the seating plan today. Buy everything that keeps: canned goods, baking supplies, drinks, paper goods and decorations. Check the oven, the roasting pan and the thermometer.
- Monday, November 23: shop for fresh produce and dairy. Start any dish that keeps for two days, such as cranberry sauce.
- Tuesday, November 24: bake pies and prep vegetables. Chill drinks and clear fridge space for the turkey and the leftovers.
- Wednesday, November 25: finish cooking prep and set the table. This is the last full day before Thanksgiving, so keep the list short: pies in the oven, vegetables prepped, table set.
- Thursday, November 26: Thanksgiving. Cook the turkey, serve, and rest.

The timeline shrinks fast if the turkey is still frozen on Monday. That is the one mistake that cannot be fixed on Wednesday, which is why the thaw times below come first in practice.

## Turkey thaw and cooking countdown

The US Department of Agriculture gives two safe thaw methods, and the fridge method is the one to use with four days left.

| Turkey size | Fridge thaw | Cold water thaw |
|---|---|---|
| 12 pounds | 3 days | 6 hours |
| 16 pounds | 4 days | 8 hours |
| 20 pounds | 4 to 5 days | 10 hours |

The fridge rule is about 1 day per 4 to 5 pounds, so a 12 pound turkey needs 3 days and a 16 pound turkey needs 4 days. A 20 pound turkey needs 4 to 5 days, so it should be in the fridge by today, November 22, at the latest. The cold water rule is 30 minutes per pound, with the water changed every 30 minutes, so a 12 pound turkey takes 6 hours and a 16 pound turkey takes 8 hours. Cold water is the fallback for a turkey that is still frozen on Wednesday, not the plan.

Cook the turkey to 165 F internal temperature, measured in the thickest part of the breast and the thigh. Let it rest for 20 minutes before carving, and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

## What is open and closed on Thanksgiving

Thanksgiving Day is a US federal holiday, so federal offices, post offices and banks are closed, and the New York Stock Exchange is closed.

Grocery stores are the exception most people need. Many stay open with reduced hours on Thanksgiving Day, usually closing in the early afternoon, but hours vary by chain and by store. Pharmacies follow the same pattern: some locations open with holiday hours, others closed. Restaurants are mixed: many are closed, some serve a Thanksgiving meal by reservation, and fast food hours vary by franchise. Do the shopping by Wednesday, November 25, and treat Thursday as a day with no errands.

## The day after: Black Friday

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving. It is not a federal holiday, so federal offices reopen, banks reopen, and the stock market runs a shortened session. Many private employers treat it as a day off even though the government does not.

For planning, the point is the weekend shape. Thanksgiving Thursday, Black Friday, and the weekend give four days off for many workers, which is why travel peaks on Wednesday, November 25, and again on Sunday, November 29. If you are driving home, Sunday is the return peak, not Friday.
