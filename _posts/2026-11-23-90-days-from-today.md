## What is 90 days from today?

Ninety days from Monday, November 23, 2026 is **Sunday, February 21, 2027**. The count starts from tomorrow, November 24, and runs through February 21. It is 12 full weeks plus 6 days, which is why the weekday shifts: Monday plus 6 days lands on a Sunday.

## 90 days from today: the quarter breakdown

Ninety days is a full quarter, which is why it is the standard length for plans, trials and goals. The span crosses four calendar months and two calendar years.

| Month | Days in the span |
|---|---|
| November 2026 | 7 days (November 24 to 30) |
| December 2026 | 31 days |
| January 2027 | 31 days |
| February 2027 | 21 days |
| Total | 90 days |

The quarter starts in late November, runs through the whole of December and January, and ends in the third week of February. That shape is useful for planning: the first month holds the holidays, the middle month is the longest clean stretch, and the final three weeks are the run-in to the deadline.

## Business days in the next 90 days

The span holds 64 weekdays. Five of them are US federal holidays, which leaves **59 US business days**.

| Month | Weekdays | Federal holidays | Business days |
|---|---|---|---|
| November 2026 | 5 | Thanksgiving (Nov 26) | 4 |
| December 2026 | 23 | Christmas Day (Dec 25) | 22 |
| January 2027 | 21 | New Years Day (Jan 1), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Jan 19) | 19 |
| February 2027 | 15 | Presidents Day (Feb 15) | 14 |
| Total | 64 | 5 | 59 |

If your deadline is a work deadline, 59 is the number to plan around, not 90. December is the biggest working month in the span with 22 business days, and November has only 4 because the count starts on November 23 and Thanksgiving falls in the first week. ## 90-day milestones

A 90 day plan works best in four checkpoints. Each one is a fixed distance from November 23, 2026.

| Checkpoint | Date | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days | December 23, 2026 | Wednesday |
| 45 days | January 7, 2027 | Thursday |
| 60 days | January 22, 2027 | Friday |
| 75 days | February 6, 2027 | Saturday |
| 90 days | February 21, 2027 | Sunday |

The 30 day mark lands two days before Christmas, the 60 day mark lands on a Friday in late January, and the final date is a Sunday, so a Monday February 22 start is the practical first working day after the deadline. There are 13 Fridays in the span, one per week plus one extra, which makes a Friday check-in a clean weekly rhythm.

## 90 days vs 3 months

Ninety days and three months are close but not the same. Three calendar months from November 23, 2026 is Tuesday, February 23, 2027, two days after the 90 day date. The gap exists because December and January have 31 days each, so three calendar months in this span run 92 days, not 90.

Use 90 days when the contract, trial or plan is written in days. Use 3 months when the agreement is written in calendar months. Mixing the two is a common source of missed deadlines: a "90 day" free trial that starts November 23 ends February 21, while a "3 month" trial that starts the same day ends February 23. ## How to calculate it yourself

Add 90 to the start date and read the result off the calendar. From November 23, 2026: 7 days take you to November 30, 31 more take you to December 31, 31 more take you to January 31, and the last 21 take you to February 21, 2027. The running total is 7 + 31 + 31 + 21 = 90.

In Excel or Google Sheets, put the start date in A1 and use `=A1+90`, then format the result as a date. To count only working days, use `=WORKDAY(A1,59)` for 59 US business days, then remove the five federal holidays with a holiday list.
