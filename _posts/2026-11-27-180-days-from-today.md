**180 days from today is Wednesday, May 26, 2027.** That is 25 weeks and 5 days from Friday, November 27, 2026, and the span holds 128 weekdays, 52 weekend days and 124 US business days. Half a year is long enough to finish a real goal and short enough to plan in detail, so this post gives the exact date, the month by month count, the business days, and a simple six-month planning method. For any other start date, run the same count on our [Days From Today Calculator]({{ '/days-from-today-calculator/' | relative_url }}) and enter 180.

## What is 180 days from today?

Counted from Friday, November 27, 2026, 180 days later is Wednesday, May 26, 2027. Today is not counted, so the count starts on November 28 and ends on May 26. The month by month split adds up to exactly 180:

| Month | Days counted |
|---|---|
| November 2026 (Nov 28 to 30) | 3 |
| December 2026 | 31 |
| January 2027 | 31 |
| February 2027 | 28 |
| March 2027 | 31 |
| April 2027 | 30 |
| May 2027 (May 1 to 26) | 26 |
| **Total** | **180** |

December, January and March are the three 31-day months in the span, and February 2027 is the shortest at 28 days, because 2027 is not a leap year. That short February is also why 180 days is one day less than six calendar months, which is covered below.

## How many weeks, weekdays and business days?

180 days breaks down as 25 full weeks plus 5 days. Inside that span there are 128 weekdays and 52 weekend days. The 52 weekend days are 26 Saturdays and 26 Sundays.

For work planning, the number that matters is business days. There are 124 US business days between November 27, 2026 and May 26, 2027. The 128 weekdays lose 4 days to US federal holidays in the span:

| Federal holiday | Date | Day of the week |
|---|---|---|
| Christmas Day | December 25, 2026 | Friday |
| New Year's Day | January 1, 2027 | Friday |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day | January 18, 2027 | Monday |
| Presidents Day | February 15, 2027 | Monday |

Two of the four holidays fall on a Friday, so they create two long weekends in the span. If your workplace also closes on other days, such as Christmas Eve, check your own calendar, because those are not federal holidays and are not removed from the 124 count. ## 180 days vs 6 months: the one-day gap

180 days and six months sound like the same thing, but they are not. Six calendar months from November 27, 2026 is Thursday, May 27, 2027, which is one day after the 180-day date of Wednesday, May 26, 2027.

The gap comes from the months in the span. Six calendar months from late November to late May covers December, January, February, March, April and May. February 2027 has only 28 days, so the six-month span is 181 days long, not 180. In a span that included a 31-day February, which never happens, or a leap-year February with 29 days, the gap would be different, so always check which one your deadline means. A contract that says 180 days and a plan that says six months can point at different dates, and the one-day difference matters for a deadline.

## Half-year planning: how to use 180 days

Half a year is the sweet spot for a goal that is too big for a month and too vague for a year. The method below splits the 180 days into six 30-day blocks, with a review at the end of each block.

**Set one outcome, not six.** Pick a single result for May 26, 2027, such as a finished project, a savings total, or a skill learned. One outcome keeps the plan simple enough to follow for six months.

**Work in 30-day blocks.** Each block is one month of work with one milestone:

| Block | Ends | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 30 | December 27, 2026 (Sunday) | First habits in place |
| Days 31 to 60 | January 26, 2027 (Tuesday) | First real progress to show |
| Days 61 to 90 | February 25, 2027 (Thursday) | Halfway point, honest review |
| Days 91 to 120 | March 27, 2027 (Saturday) | Past the halfway slump |
| Days 121 to 150 | April 26, 2027 (Monday) | Final push starts |
| Days 151 to 180 | May 26, 2027 (Wednesday) | Goal reached |

**Review at the halfway point.** February 25, 2027 is day 90, exactly halfway. That is the review that matters most. If you are behind at day 90, cut the scope, not the deadline. If you are ahead, bring the finish forward instead of adding new work.

**Plan around the holidays.** The span crosses Christmas, New Year, Martin Luther King Jr. Day and Presidents Day. Those weeks are slow for most people, so put light work there and heavy work in March and April, the two full months with no federal holiday in the span.

**Use weekly check-ins.** 25 full weeks means 25 weekly check-ins. A 15-minute check each week, on the same weekday, is enough to catch a slip before it becomes a missed deadline.

## Worked examples

**Example 1: a savings goal.** Saving $10 a day for 180 days gives $1,800 by May 26, 2027. Saving $10 on each of the 124 business days gives $1,240. Pick the daily version if you want a steady habit, or the business-day version if income only arrives on workdays.

**Example 2: a project deadline.** A project that starts November 27, 2026 and must ship May 26, 2027 has 124 business days of work. If the team works in two-week sprints, that is about 12 full sprints plus a 4-day finish. The sprint that covers Christmas week will be short, so plan it as a cleanup sprint, not a delivery sprint.

**Example 3: a learning goal.** Studying 30 minutes a day for 180 days is 90 hours, which is enough for a solid beginner level in a new skill. Studying only on the 128 weekdays gives about 64 hours. The daily habit wins by 26 hours, which is why a small daily habit beats a bigger weekday-only plan over six months.

**Example 4: a countdown to an event.** If an event falls on May 26, 2027, then November 27, 2026 is the day to start a 180-day countdown. Booking travel or a venue 180 days out usually means better prices and more choice than booking 90 days out, and the [Countdown Timer]({{ '/countdown-timer/' | relative_url }}) can run the live count from today.

## How to count 180 days yourself

The quickest manual method is weeks first, then days. Add 25 weeks to the start date, then add 5 more days.

From November 27, 2026: 25 weeks lands on Friday, May 21, 2027. Add 5 days: Saturday May 22, Sunday May 23, Monday May 24, Tuesday May 25, Wednesday May 26, 2027.

For any other start date, the same method works, but watch out for February and for leap years, which change the month split.
