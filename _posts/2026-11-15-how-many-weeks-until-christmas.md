## How many weeks until Christmas 2026?

The short answer, worked from November 22, 2026, is 4 full weeks and 5 days, or 33 days in total. That is the number most people want, and the rest of this guide breaks it down, because a countdown and a plan are not the same thing: the calendar says 33 days, but only 23 of the days left are business days, and only 8 are weekend days.

The week count is the same in the US, UK and Canada: the calendar does not change by country. What changes is which days count as business days, and that is covered below.

## Full weeks vs leftover days: how the count works

Divide the days left by 7. The whole number is the full weeks, and the remainder is the leftover days. From November 22, 2026: 33 days divided by 7 is 4, with 5 left over. So the answer is 4 full weeks and 5 days.

The same method works from any date. From December 1, 2026, Christmas is 24 days away. 24 divided by 7 is 3, with 3 left over, so that is 3 full weeks and 3 days. Check any date with our [Date Difference Calculator]({{ '/date-difference-calculator/' | relative_url }}): enter the date and December 25, 2026, and it gives the exact day count.

A common mistake is to round up. 33 days is not 5 weeks. It is 4 full weeks and 5 days, and the 5 leftover days matter, because they hold the last weekend before Christmas.

## Days, weekends and Fridays left

Here is the span from November 23 to December 25, 2026, broken down:

| Count | Days |
|---|---|
| Calendar days until Christmas Day | 33 |
| Days before Christmas (to December 24) | 32 |
| Weekdays (Monday to Friday) | 25 |
| Weekend days (Saturday and Sunday) | 8 |
| Fridays strictly between now and Christmas | 4 |

The 4 Fridays are November 27, December 4, December 11 and December 18. Christmas Day itself is also a Friday, so there are 5 Fridays in the span if you count it. Fridays matter because they are payday for many people paid weekly or every two weeks, and they are the natural day to finish the week's Christmas shopping.

The 8 weekend days are 4 Saturdays and 4 Sundays: November 28 and 29, December 5 and 6, December 12 and 13, and December 19 and 20. If your shopping happens on weekends, that is 4 shopping weekends left, including the last one, December 19 and 20.

## Business days left

The business day count is 23. Start with the 24 weekdays between November 23 and December 24, 2026, then remove Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 26. Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, is a US federal holiday, so federal offices close on the day itself. That leaves 23 business days.

Weekdays and business days are not the same thing. A weekday is any Monday to Friday. A business day is a weekday minus public holidays. Thanksgiving is the only weekday holiday in the span besides Christmas Day itself, so the difference here is small, but it is the difference that decides whether a delivery arrives on time. Count business days for shipping, time off and deadlines, and count calendar days only for the countdown itself. Read our guide [How to Count Business Days Between Two Dates]({{ '/blog/how-to-count-business-days-between-two-dates/' | relative_url }}) for the full method, or count it with our [Business Days Calculator]({{ '/business-days-calculator/' | relative_url }}).

A parcel that needs 5 business days and is sent on Monday, December 21, 2026 uses December 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28, because Christmas Day is a federal holiday and December 26 and 27 are weekend days. Federal holidays come from the [Office of Personnel Management](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/).

## Christmas Eve and the days after

Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24, 2026, which is 32 days after November 22, 2026. It is the last full day for shopping, cooking and travel before Christmas Day, and it is a normal working day for most people: it is not a federal holiday.

Advent in 2026 begins on Sunday, November 29, 2026, the fourth Sunday before Christmas. If you follow an advent calendar, that is the day the countdown starts in earnest.

The countdown ends on Christmas Day, but the season does not. New Year follows 7 days later, on Friday, January 1, 2027, the same weekday as Christmas Day. If you are planning past Christmas, that is the next date to count to.

## Christmas dates 2026 to 2030

Christmas always falls on December 25, but the weekday moves every year:

| Year | Christmas Day | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | December 25 | Friday |
| 2027 | December 25 | Saturday |
| 2028 | December 25 | Monday |
| 2029 | December 25 | Tuesday |
| 2030 | December 25 | Wednesday |

Christmas 2027 falls on a Saturday, which means the federal observance moves to Friday, December 24, 2027. Christmas 2028 falls on a Monday, the first Monday Christmas since 2023.

## Week by week plan to Christmas

Four checkpoints, one per Friday, from now to Christmas Day:

| Checkpoint | Date | Weeks to go | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friday | November 27 | 4 weeks to go | Order gifts that ship. November 27 is Black Friday in 2026. |
| Friday | December 4 | 3 weeks to go | Send parcels. Check your carrier's Christmas cut-off before you ship. |
| Friday | December 11 | 2 weeks to go | Wrap gifts and confirm travel plans. |
| Friday | December 18 | 1 week to go | Final shopping, groceries and cooking prep. |

The plan works because each checkpoint is a Friday, the same day the week count drops. Miss a Friday and the next one is a week closer.
