On November 22, 2026, there are **39 days left in the year**. December 31, 2026 is 39 days away, and New Year 2027 is 40 days away.

That count was right on November 22, and it drops by one every day. For the live number right now, use our [Countdown Timer]({{ '/countdown-timer/' | relative_url }}), which ticks down to midnight in real time.

## How many days are left in the year?

Short answer: 39 days, as of Sunday, November 22, 2026.

The year ends on Thursday, December 31, 2026, which is 39 days away. New Year's Day, Friday, January 1, 2027, is 40 days away.

Why two numbers? Because January 1 itself is the extra day. There are 39 days left in 2026 through December 31, and 40 days until January 1. If a page says 40 days left in the year, it is counting to New Year, not to the end of this year. If it says 39 days until New Year, it is counting to New Year's Eve. Both are right for different questions. This post keeps them apart so you never mix them up.

## Days left vs days until New Year

Search results show both numbers side by side with no explanation. Here is the rule in one line:

- Days left in the year: count to December 31. On November 22, that is 39.
- Days until New Year: count to January 1. On November 22, that is 40.
- Days until New Year's Eve: count to December 31. On November 22, that is also 39, the same as days left in the year.

The same rule works from any date. From December 1, 2026, there are 30 days left in the year and 31 days until New Year. From Christmas Day, December 25, there are 6 days left in the year and 7 days until New Year, exactly one week, because Christmas and New Year fall on the same weekday this year: Friday.

Want the number for any other date? Check it with our [Date Difference Calculator]({{ '/date-difference-calculator/' | relative_url }}): enter the date and December 31, 2026, and it gives the exact day count.

## What day of the year is it today?

November 22, 2026 is day 326 of 365. That means 89.3% of the year is done and 10.7% is left. It is ISO week 47 of 53, and we are in the fourth quarter, which started on October 1.

Quote the percent when someone asks how far through the year we are. Use the day count when you are planning, because 39 days is a deadline and 89.3% is not. Both come from the same fact: 2026 is not a leap year, so it has 365 days, and November 22 is the 326th of them.

## How many weeks left in 2026?

The 39 days left break down like this:

- 5 weeks and 4 days (39 divided by 7 is 5, with 4 left over)
- 29 weekdays and 10 weekend days after today: 5 Saturdays and 5 Sundays
- 5 Fridays: November 27, December 4, December 11, December 18 and December 25

Read the Friday list carefully, because the fifth Friday is Christmas Day itself. There are 4 ordinary Fridays left before Christmas, and Christmas Day makes the fifth. One of them, November 27, is Black Friday, the day after Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 26.

Weekends are where most of the holiday planning happens, so the split matters more than the single number. Ten weekend days is five full weekends to fit in shopping, travel and family time before the year ends.

## Business days left

There are 27 US business days left in 2026 as of November 22, 2026. That is the 29 weekdays left after today, minus two federal holidays: Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 26, and Christmas Day itself, Friday, December 25.

Weekdays and business days are not the same thing. A weekday is any Monday to Friday. A business day is a weekday minus public holidays. Here the difference is only two days, but it is the difference that decides whether a delivery arrives on time or a project finishes before the holidays. Count business days for shipping, time off and deadlines, and count calendar days only for the countdown itself. Read our guide [How to Count Business Days Between Two Dates]({{ '/blog/how-to-count-business-days-between-two-dates/' | relative_url }}) for the full method, or count it with our [Business Days Calculator]({{ '/business-days-calculator/' | relative_url }}).

Two worked examples show how the holidays move a deadline. A parcel that needs 5 business days and is sent on Monday, December 21, 2026 uses December 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28, because Christmas Day is a federal holiday and December 26 and 27 are weekend days. A parcel sent on Monday, December 28 uses December 28, 29, 30, 31 and January 4, because January 1 is a federal holiday and January 2 and 3 are weekend days. Federal holidays come from the [Office of Personnel Management](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/).

## Holidays left in 2026

Only two federal holidays are left in 2026, and both fall inside the 39 days:

- Thanksgiving: Thursday, November 26, 2026
- Christmas Day: Friday, December 25, 2026

New Year's Eve, Thursday, December 31, is not a federal holiday, so it counts as a business day. New Year's Day, Friday, January 1, 2027, is a federal holiday, but it belongs to next year, so it does not reduce the 2026 business-day count. If you are planning time off, those two holidays plus the weekends around them are the only free days left in the year.

## How to work it out yourself, and in Excel

The formula is simple: days left equals the days in the year minus the day number of today. On November 22, that is 365 minus 326, which is 39.

In Excel or Google Sheets, put any date in cell A1 and use this formula for the days left after that date:

`=DAYS(DATE(YEAR(A1),12,31),A1)`

For business days, use NETWORKDAYS with your holiday list in cells H1:H2:

`=NETWORKDAYS(A1,DATE(YEAR(A1),12,31),H1:H2)`

Put Thanksgiving and Christmas Day in the holiday range, or the formula will count them as work days. The same result comes from our [Business Days Calculator]({{ '/business-days-calculator/' | relative_url }}), which already removes the federal holidays for you.
